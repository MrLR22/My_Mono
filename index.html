Hi, I'm Likhith (Mr LR).

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, graduating 2027. I build CPU-only perception systems — right now that means Mnemo, a privacy-preserving episodic memory retriever for egocentric video. Long-term, I want to get into embodied AI and robotics research, and license lightweight on-device perception to small robotics teams who can't afford a GPU budget.
