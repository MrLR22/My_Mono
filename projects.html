01

Mnemo / Engram

active

A wearable memory assistant that remembers what you did, not what you saw. A chest-worn camera feeds a small always-on model — MediaPipe Hands, YOLOv8n and sparse optical flow into HOI-TCN, a 0.24M-parameter causal temporal CNN — that decides which 4-second windows are worth a closer look. Only those go to Qwen3-VL-4B, which writes short structured lines into a text-only SQLite "reference diary". No frame is ever stored. Questions like "did I close the fridge?" are answered only from the diary, with times and places, and it declines when the record doesn't say. Over a whole 2.4-hour morning it gets 76% of questions right versus 66% for the VLM shown the entire morning, and invents no events. This is my final-year research project; the paper (ENGRAM) is in preparation.
